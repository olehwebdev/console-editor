Patch a live website without rebuilding it

  1. Type the site's URL in the bar above and press Enter.
  2. Pick a script, stylesheet or the HTML under Page resources. Minified files are pretty-printed.
  3. Edit and press Ctrl/Cmd+S. The page reloads with your version of the file.

Overrides are kept between sessions. Turn them on and off in the sidebar.

The website will appear here.
